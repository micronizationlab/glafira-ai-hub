# GLAFIRA — consumer presentation narrative (draft v1)

**Audience:** everyday users, not developers. **Goal:** make people want to try the AI agent and understand VPN/earning in plain language.

## 1. Your personal AI agent. Your privacy. Your choice.

GLAFIRA is more than a chatbot. It is a personal AI agent that can help plan and complete multi-step tasks, work with your files and approved tools, remember project context and continue work within the permissions you choose.

Use it from your phone. If you want more power, add your own computer and optional Windows Agent.

## 2. What you get

- AI agent: ask for an outcome, not just an answer. GLAFIRA can break work into steps, coordinate specialist agents and report progress.
- VPN: protect your connection with a clear on/off state and transparent service status.
- Phone-only start: install the mobile app and use GLAFIRA without installing anything at home or work.
- Your own computer, if you choose: Windows or Mac Desktop provides a control center; Windows Agent can use spare approved capacity on an additional Windows PC.
- Your account follows you: after registration, your download center prepares companion apps paired to your account.

## 3. Do I have to pay cash?

Not necessarily. Members can earn internal GLF service credits by voluntarily contributing spare device capacity to verified tasks.

If your device is suitable, work is available and your completed tasks are accepted, earned credits can cover some or potentially all of the base VPN/AI package. You can also use local models or connect your own provider account, subject to provider terms and costs.

**Honest promise:** with sufficient accepted contribution, you may cover the base service package in GLF credits instead of paying cash. Earnings vary by device, workload, available hours, energy cost, network demand and acceptance. No guaranteed daily income.

## 4. Three ways to start

1. Just my phone — register, download mobile app and start with available local/free allowances.
2. Phone + Desktop — add Windows or Mac Desktop for a larger workspace, local files and paired-device control.
3. Desktop + Windows Agent — optionally add a second Windows computer as a background worker. It uses only resources you approve and yields to your normal work.

## 5. What makes GLAFIRA different?

Do not claim generic AI agents or scheduled tools are unique. Our intended differentiation is the combination of owner-controlled local computer fleet, optional distributed compute with visible consent and auditable rewards, local/hosted/BYOK model choices, AI and VPN service credits in one transparent wallet, and the ability to pause/revoke devices. These are differentiators only after each feature exists and passes acceptance.

## 6. How earning works

1. You decide whether a device contributes.
2. GLAFIRA measures workload and device conditions.
3. The device receives only eligible tasks.
4. Completed work is checked and accepted.
5. The wallet credits the approved GLF reward after acceptance.
6. You use credits for eligible VPN/AI services.

The app shows estimated energy cost, earned credits, task history and reward basis. Idle uptime alone earns nothing.

## 7. Transparent pricing

The wallet may show an illustrative reference: 300 RUB monthly package represented by 10,000 GLF credits. This is not final until owner approval. If active-day billing is enabled, VPN is charged only when actually available and used under the published rule. No daily VPN debit when offline or unused. AI is charged by measured usage or a separately disclosed active-day package.

## 8. Short onboarding

Register once on the GLAFIRA website. Choose your first device: Windows Desktop, Mac Desktop or smartphone. Activate it with a one-time account-bound enrollment code. Your personal download page then prepares companion apps and optional Windows Agent paired to your account. If you do not want home/work installation, stop after the phone app — it remains a complete starting point.

## 9. Closing line

**A capable AI agent and a VPN — with a path to pay through contribution, not only cash. Start with your phone. Add your own devices only when you want to.**

## 10. Required disclosure

GLF is an internal service-credit/accounting unit, not a bank deposit, investment, security or guaranteed cash equivalent. Rewards depend on accepted work and demand; no fixed income/payback is promised. Local compute uses electricity and may cause wear. Provider free quotas are limited and subject to terms; GLAFIRA may pause hosted usage when an allowance is exhausted. VPN coverage, speeds, privacy/security and data-handling claims must be substantiated. Show only features in the released build; label roadmap items as planned.
