# GLAFIRA — consumer presentation narrative (draft v1)

**Your personal AI agent. Your privacy. Your choice.**

GLAFIRA is more than a chatbot. It is a personal AI agent that can plan and complete multi-step tasks, work with your files and approved tools, remember project context and continue within the permissions you choose.

### What you get
- A capable AI agent: ask for an outcome, not just an answer.
- A VPN with clear connection and service status.
- Phone-only start: no home/work installation required.
- Optional Windows or Mac Desktop for a larger workspace.
- Optional Windows Agent on another Windows PC, using only capacity you approve.

### Do I have to pay cash?
Not necessarily. Members can earn internal GLF service credits by voluntarily contributing spare device capacity to verified tasks. If the device is suitable, work is available and tasks are accepted, credits may cover some or potentially all of the base VPN/AI package.

**Honest promise:** with enough accepted contribution, you may cover the base service package in GLF credits instead of paying cash. Earnings vary by device, workload, hours, energy cost, network demand and acceptance. No guaranteed daily income.

### Three ways to start
1. Just my phone.
2. Phone + Windows or Mac Desktop.
3. Desktop + optional Windows Agent.

### How earning works
You opt in → device is benchmarked → eligible task is assigned → result is checked and accepted → approved GLF reward is credited → you spend credits on eligible VPN/AI services. Idle uptime alone earns nothing.

### Transparent pricing
Illustrative reference only: 300 RUB monthly package represented by 10,000 GLF credits. Final value and tariff require owner approval. VPN active-day debit only when actually available and used under published rules. No daily VPN debit when offline or unused. AI is charged by measured usage or a clearly disclosed active-day plan.

### Closing
**A capable AI agent and a VPN — with a path to pay through contribution, not only cash. Start with your phone. Add your own devices only when you want to.**

### Disclosure
GLF is an internal service-credit/accounting unit, not an investment, security, deposit or guaranteed cash equivalent. No fixed income/payback is promised. Local compute consumes electricity and may cause wear. Provider free quotas are limited and subject to provider terms. Show only released features; mark roadmap items as planned.
